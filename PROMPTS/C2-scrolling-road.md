# C2 · The core illusion

**Claude Code**

```
The car and the camera never move. Ever.

Instead, move the road and roadside geometry toward the camera along negative
Z. Road segments are 9 metres long and tile seamlessly at that interval — keep
enough of them alive to cover the visible distance, and recycle each one to the
back of the queue the moment it passes behind the camera.

Expose the speed as a single named constant at the top of the file.

No controls, no obstacles yet.
```
