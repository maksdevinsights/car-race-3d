# T6 · The verification loop

**Claude Code**

```
Set up a verification loop you can run yourself, without me in it.

Write a Playwright script that boots the dev server, drives a scripted route
through the town — a lap, two turns, one deliberate collision — and captures
screenshots at fixed points along the way.

Then run it, open the screenshots, and look at them. Write VERIFY.md listing
every visual problem you can see: clipping, camera going through geometry,
buildings floating, z-fighting, the car sitting at the wrong height, anything.

Rank them by how bad they look, not by how hard they are to fix.

Then fix the top three and re-run the loop to confirm they're gone.

Don't ask me what's broken. Look at the screenshots and tell me.
```
