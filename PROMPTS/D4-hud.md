# D4 · The HUD

**Claude Design**

```
Design the game interface as a flat 2D overlay, not 3D:

- Score, top left
- Current speed, top right
- Three life icons, bottom left
- A start screen with the game title and a play button
- A game-over card with the final score and a retry button

Use the design system. Score and speed in Anton, #E6EDF3. Labels in JetBrains
Mono uppercase, #8B949E. Life icons in #F85149. Buttons are #F85149 fills with
#0D1117 text, 8px corner radius.

Keep it sparse and high contrast, readable at a glance while something is
moving underneath it. Accent used once per screen, no more.
```
